About Me

I'm currently a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, under the supervision of Prof. Lei Ying.
Before joining the University of Michigan, I received my Ph.D. degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the
University of British Columbia, where I had the privilege of being advised by Prof. Lele Wang.

My research develops theoretical foundations and efficient algorithms for statistical inference and learning in structured
and networked systems. Using tools from information theory, probability, and high-dimensional statistics, I study how network
structure, auxiliary information, and noisy observations shape the statistical and computational limits of reliable inference.
